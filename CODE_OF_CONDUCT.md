# Contributor conduct

Participation in this project requires respectful, constructive behavior.
Critique code and evidence, not people's identity or personal circumstances.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and publication of private
material are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or camera-derived personal
information, even as part of a disagreement or bug report.

The project follows the principles and proportionate enforcement approach of
the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This short project policy does not reproduce or relicense that external text.

Maintainers may edit/remove abusive or privacy-violating content, restrict
participation and document a reason without repeating sensitive material.
Warnings, temporary restrictions and permanent removal should be proportionate
to the behavior and repeated incidents. The repository owner's authority does
not exempt the owner or assistants from this policy.

For platform abuse, use [GitHub's private reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not litigate private accusations in a public issue. If a report concerns a
security or privacy defect in this software, use the private vulnerability route
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). These routes are not emergency services.
